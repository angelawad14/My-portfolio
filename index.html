Design process · How I work

How a project runs

  1. Understand

    I start by listening. I map the business goals, the users and the constraints, so the team agrees on the real problem before any design begins.

  2. Research & Define

    Interviews, data and a review of the market turn into clear requirements, user journeys and success measures that guide every decision.

  3. AI Design

    Where it helps, I design AI into the flow: assistants, recommendations and automation with transparency, human control and graceful errors.

  4. Validate

    Prototypes are tested with real users and options are compared with experiments, so decisions rest on evidence, not opinion.

  5. Deliver

    Handoff-ready screens, a documented design system and close work with engineers, so the product ships as designed and can scale.
